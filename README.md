# How to Use

<p align="center" style="text-align: center">
   <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CodeRedOnly/WttM/refs/heads/main/assets/images/About.png" alt="WttM Logo" />
</p>

On the screen, you will see three ***Values*** and three ***Resources***.

Choose one of the values, and one of the resources.  They do not need to come from the same displayed card.  Write your value on your card, and use
the resource's benefit.

Pay attention to the ***Next Resources***.  They will be the next drawn resources, and can help prepare your strategy for the following draw.

Once all players have marked their card, press the ***Draw Next*** button to pull the next three cards.

If you accidentally double hit the draw button, or accidentally draw while someone is still choosing, you can hit the ***Previous*** button to view the history of drawn cards.

The deck will be reshuffled automatically as draws continue. If at any point you want to completely reset the deck, you can press the ***Shuffle*** button.

**Note:** Reshuffling removes all stored history.  
**Note:** Shuffling returns the discard pile to the deck, which will affect overall card draw statistics if used frequently.
